Provider Enrollment · Specialist Workbench · Concept v0.1

The enrollment case file, operationalized.

Camunda 8 orchestrates provider enrollment end-to-end — intake, exclusion screening, type-specific verification, SA/AO referral, eligibility, and the CMS decision. This prototype shows the other half of the system: the people inside it. Not a process visualizer, and not a generic task list — a specialist’s desk where every assignment arrives with its reason, its deadline, its evidence, and the workflow position, ready to be worked.

Open the workbench → 5 screens · fully clickable · simulated data from the project’s Testing Guide scenarios

Design principles

What makes this a specialist’s workbench rather than another Camunda front-end.

The work, not the tooling

Screens are organized around what the specialist does — decide, verify, file, release — never around Camunda primitives like “process instances” or “element templates”.

Attention is the interface

A priority-ranked queue where every item states its reason (a pending decision, a closing window, a compliance flag) and its deadline. Nothing hides behind filters.

Process context beside the work

Every case shows where it truly stands in the BPMN journey — what automation already did, which gateway branched, and what the specialist’s action unlocks next.

A federal visual language

Warm paper, ink navy, tracking numbers in monospace, stamp states, hairline ledgers — the case file aesthetic of PECOS and CMS-855, made operational.

Grounded in this project’s workflows

Every name, form field, and stage in these mockups comes from the deployed artifacts — nothing invented. Phase 2 ports the design onto the existing Angular app + Go BFF.
